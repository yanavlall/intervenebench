# Role-Focused Evaluation Program v1

**Status:** frozen productization pivot. This decision authorizes no model calls,
paid compute, human-outcome access, participant-row access, or schema-only
participant-container inspection.

## Decision

InterveneBench will stop open-ended corpus expansion and will not require a new
12--16 experiment panel for project completion. The primary deliverable is now a
reproducible behavioral-simulator evaluation and release-gating system, backed
by the completed nine-experiment development record and six-experiment
outcome-sealed prospective confirmation.

This changes the allocation of future effort, not the interpretation of past
evidence. The six-experiment result remains a small, noncanonical prospective
case study. The project will not relabel it as a universal benchmark or use the
pivot to tune a trust threshold after outcome reveal.

## Completion target

The finished system must provide one coherent lifecycle from evaluation-spec
validation through simulator execution, prediction freeze, authorized outcome
reveal, scoring, model-version comparison, scoped release decision, and a
replayable aggregate-only report.

Release decisions must distinguish autonomous intervention selection,
research-stage candidate screening, confidence-based abstention, and
small-sample human fallback. Evidence for one scope cannot authorize another.

## Bounded extension

Only `wr7jg` may continue as a new behavioral extension because its downstream
endpoint is matched voter-file turnout rather than another self-reported survey
attitude. Its schema-only mapping remains separately unauthorized and has a
four-hour completion cap once explicitly opened. Failure to resolve it safely
does not block the role-focused project.

The earlier independent-replication completion queue remains as historical
planning evidence but is closed without execution. `9tv2b`, `dvwu7`, and
`6wbd7` engineering artifacts may remain in the repository; they do not reopen
corpus expansion or alter the completed prospective case study.

## Claim boundary

The product may demonstrate evaluation design, model regression testing,
uncertainty handling, provenance, access boundaries, and evidence-to-release
reasoning. It may report the existing prospective results exactly as frozen.
It may not claim general simulator trustworthiness, a validated confidence
model, autonomous deployment readiness, or successful limited-human fallback.

The machine-readable authority is
`data/manifests/research/role_focused_evaluation_program_v1.json`.
