# Independent Replication Completion Queue v1

**Status:** frozen outcome-blind engineering queue. No model calls, paid compute,
participant-row access, or human-outcome reveal are authorized.

## Decision

Open-ended corpus search is over. The project now converts the finite set of
already identified scientific survivors into runnable tasks. The first wave is
`9tv2b`, `dvwu7`, `6wbd7`, `h6txz`, `wr7jg`, and `a42yg`; nine previously
identified conditionals form a bounded second wave.

The ordering uses source readiness, mapping completeness, adapter reuse, and
bounded engineering cost. It uses no human outcome, treatment effect, winner,
or simulator result.

## Completion rule

Every task must independently pass source identity, fielding and paradigm
deduplication, fixed-world deployable actions, bounded monotone utility, exact
or explicitly labeled source-faithful reconstruction, outcome-blind human
mapping, and tested adapter coverage.

The replication proceeds only with at least 12 runnable tasks, including at
least eight SocSci210 tasks. If the 15-row queue cannot supply that panel, stop
before inference or reveal and report corpus insufficiency. Do not add another
corpus or relax the gates.

Schema-only inspection of a participant container is not authorized by this
queue. If a mapping cannot be completed from codebooks and public design files,
prepare a separate narrow request defining the exact fields and zero-outcome
boundary.

The authoritative queue is
`data/manifests/research/independent_replication_completion_queue_v1.json`.
