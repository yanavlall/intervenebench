# Portfolio Pilot Scope Decision

## Decision

InterveneBench will first produce a finished five-experiment portfolio study. The publication-scale trust benchmark remains a documented extension, not the minimum deliverable.

The five experiments are `5vm8g`, `xc4yq`, `de5hx`, `turagaS11`, and `wallaceS12`. Their recommendations were frozen while outcomes were sealed. A separate development-reveal decision subsequently authorized only their declared outcomes and permanently made the five development-only. The other six runnable contracts remain sealed.

This decision does not weaken the benchmark methodology. It narrows the immediate claim:

> On five source-verified randomized decision tasks, do frozen synthetic recommendations reproduce human intervention rankings, and what regret follows when they do not?

The pilot may establish concrete successes, failures, treatment-effect errors, and regret on these tasks. It may not validate a general trust model or broad cross-paradigm reliability.

## Authorized Work

- Run the frozen no-effect policy.
- Run local `llama3.2:3b` under `DESIGN_ONLY` access.
- Save strict structured outputs, synthetic effects, recommendations, and outcome-free diagnostics.
- Freeze and verify artifacts before any human result is accessible.

## Not Authorized by the Original Response-Free Scope

- Human-outcome reveal or scoring without a separate authorization.
- Paid inference or Modal.
- Fine-tuning or cloud participant-data transmission.
- Fitting a real-outcome classical baseline.
- A canonical train/validation/test assignment.
- A prospective trust-model claim.

## Role of the Five Experiments

Running the simulators does not remove these experiments from the benchmark. If their human outcomes are later revealed under a separate decision, they become reusable development evidence in any larger benchmark. They cease only to be untouched test experiments.

## Expansion Gate

The completed development result contains meaningful descriptive-versus-decision divergence and informative failure cases. Expansion to the six still-sealed runnable contracts or another corpus is nevertheless optional: it is justified only if the project now aims for a stronger prospective trust claim, not merely because expansion appeared in the original long-term plan.

The response-free authority is `data/manifests/benchmark/portfolio_pilot_scope.json`. The separate reveal authority is `data/manifests/benchmark/portfolio_pilot_development_reveal.json`, and the completed empirical interpretation is in `docs/reports/portfolio_pilot_development_results.md`.
