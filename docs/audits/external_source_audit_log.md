# External Source-Audit Log

This log applies the InterveneBench source, action-set, utility, and leakage rules to the frozen first external universe in `data/manifests/audits/external_candidate_universe_v1.csv`. Candidates are audited in `freeze_order`. The current machine-readable decisions are in `data/manifests/audits/external_source_audit_registry.csv`; source hashes are in `data/manifests/audits/external_source_bundles.csv`.

This is a design and measurement audit. Human response records, arm summaries, treatment effects, significance tests, intervention winners, simulator outputs, and regret must not be intentionally consulted. If published result text is incidentally encountered while locating source materials, the exposure is logged immediately and the candidate cannot enter an untouched evaluation stratum. An external study becomes scientifically usable only if its randomized cells can be mapped to a coherent set of actions available to a declared decision-maker and a source-grounded decision utility.

Source retrieval now runs in bounded three-candidate batches under `docs/protocol/external_audit_batch_protocol.md`. Evidence collection is parallel, but leakage review, eligibility adjudication, and registry commits remain centralized and follow `freeze_order`.

## Parallel batch 5: frozen orders 15–17

This fully sealed batch yielded the cleanest external decision task so far, one measurement-only exclusion, and one exact SocSci210 duplicate. No participant file, response-rate outcome artifact, or result-bearing text was opened.

### `KrupnikovS34`: ineligible measurement experiment

The four randomized packages cross “think” versus “feel” wording for partisan self-identification and identity importance. The catalogued partisanship response is the wording-manipulated measurement question, not a downstream decision outcome. Neither partisan placement nor identity importance has a source-grounded monotone utility or stable beneficiary. Decision: `ineligible`, reason `measurement_wording_treatment_no_post_intervention_outcome_or_stable_utility`.

### `turagaS11`: eligible sealed core-simple extension

All participants receive the same mercury-risk passage. The three randomized policies add no statistic, a community contamination/severity statistic, or a household-responsibility statistic. The facts can coexist, so the arms choose truthful emphasis under a fixed world and form a coherent action set for an environmental-health communicator.

The frozen questionnaire-order primary is Q6a willingness to write in support of stronger mercury-control policies, a 1–5 ordinal outcome with higher better for the declared communication objective. Before scoring, stored-variable and missing-code mapping, weighting, realized support, and historical truth-context language must be recovered without using result distributions. Decision: `eligible_extension`, track `core_simple`, outcome access sealed.

### `SchaadS62`: exact duplicate and independently ineligible

The official OSF node is exactly SocSci210 study `9nphm`, so this row cannot add an independent experiment. Independently, its cells alter Jaime's assigned sex, current gender identity, and childhood biography rather than actions available to a stable decision-maker. Decision: exclude, reason `duplicate_of_socsci210_9nphm`; outcome access sealed.

## Parallel batch 4: frozen orders 12–14

Freeze order 11 (`system_threat`) remains a separate duplicate-adjudication hold. The next three clear candidates were audited directly and remained outcome sealed. No participant file or result-bearing text was opened.

### `Craig735`: distinct fielding, ineligible

This 2016 three-arm experiment is not a duplicate of `system_threat`/SocSci210 `345ms`, although both belong in the demographic-change/status-threat paradigm and must remain grouped at splitting. It randomizes Hispanic-growth, Asian-growth, or unrelated Census mobility press releases.

The messages are technically deployable, but the archived immigration, defense, interrogation, threat, and ideology outcomes have no source-declared beneficiary or defensible monotone decision utility. Selecting a direction would invent a political sponsor after the fact. Decision: `ineligible`, reason `ideologically_contested_policy_outcomes_no_defensible_stable_utility`.

### `Iles1294`: recoverable outcome, invalid action set

This participant-randomized 2×4 text factorial crosses a red-meat recommendation direction with four structures of scientific conflict. The source-mapped confusion outcome is clean and lower-is-better for a public-health communication objective.

The action set nevertheless fails: cells change the actual study evidence, expert positions, and recommendation rather than communication choices under one fixed factual world. No subset preserves the evidence state while varying only a truthful deployable message. Decision: `ineligible`, reason `message_cells_change_factual_evidence_state_not_deployable_actions`.

### `Blair1131`: eligible sealed action-subset extension

The full experiment contains 24 cells crossing leader gender, president party and gender, and three crisis actions. Eight three-cell subsets hold the context fixed and vary only the crisis strategy. Under the already frozen deterministic arm-subset rule, source cells 1–3 are retained: one male foreign leader and one Republican male-president context, with stay out, threaten then back down, or threaten then engage.

Source Q3 is a seven-level public-approval outcome. Higher is better only for a narrowly declared incumbent-reputation objective; it is not national-security, humanitarian, or public-welfare utility. Alternative context cells cannot become separate experiments. Before scoring, name randomization, party-blocked estimation, and weighting must be frozen. Decision: `eligible_extension`, track `extension_factorial_action_subset`, outcome access sealed.

## Parallel batch 3: frozen orders 8–10

This batch retained two scientifically usable but non-core extensions and excluded one study. Mixed archives were used only to isolate design materials; participant-data members were never extracted or opened, and every temporary archive was removed.

### `Krupnikov719`: ineligible

The final instrument randomizes three disclosure policies before political questions: no public-data disclosure, public-data disclosure with anonymity assurance, or the same disclosure plus an anti-fraud rationale. Respondents then decide whether to continue, so substantive outcomes are observed only after a treatment-affected gate.

Groups 2 and 3 form a truthful deployable wording subset for a survey researcher, but the archive-catalogued abortion, racial-attitude, and income outcomes have no defensible monotone survey-quality utility. Later political-knowledge accuracy is more objective, yet complete-case scoring would condition on post-treatment continuation. The source provides no joint participation-and-accuracy utility that resolves the participant-autonomy and researcher-yield tradeoff. Decision: `ineligible`, reason `no_source_verified_stable_utility_and_post_treatment_selection`.

No participant record was opened. A pre-field proposal unexpectedly contained prior-pilot results, and the allowed response-rate report contained target arm-level process statistics. Neither was used; the exposure is logged and the row is barred from untouched evaluation.

### `ShannonS2`: eligible sealed extension

This participant-randomized 3×2 factorial crosses a beneficiary cue (none, immigrant, or illegal immigrant) with numeric savings information (absent or present). The same six-arm message policy is applied across child-tax-credit, WIC, and in-state-tuition vignettes shown in independently randomized order.

The independently curated archive outcome and source instrument identify the child-tax-credit agreement item as the single primary endpoint: a five-level ordinal measure, oriented higher only for a declared outreach communicator's support objective. All six arms remain in the action set. WIC and tuition are repeated contexts from the same experiment and cannot create additional experiments. Before scoring, the project must freeze an estimator that marginalizes over the programmed six-order distribution, verify the item and missing-code mapping, and declare weighted or released-sample estimation.

Decision: `eligible_extension`, track `extension_factorial_repeated_message`. Outcome access remains sealed: no participant record, result text, arm summary, effect, or winner was opened.

### `AnsonBRIEF60`: eligible development-only extension

After respondents choose a preferred economic-news topic, the experiment randomizes a policy that serves a positive story matched to that preference versus an unrelated Verizon story. This is a coherent personalized intervention policy, not three separate treatment arms. A communicator can choose the policy while each synthetic persona retains the same pre-treatment topic preference under both assigned arms.

The source-primary outcome is five-level overall-economy positivity, with higher values preferred only for a narrow persuasion objective rather than truth, accuracy, or public welfare. Before scoring, the project must reconcile one routing-label discrepancy, recover response and weight coding, and freeze how to marginalize over an independently randomized colocated module that occurs before measurement.

Decision: `eligible_extension`, track `extension_personalized_policy`, development-only. No participant record was opened. Prior-pilot result text was incidentally exposed in the proposal and was not used.

## Parallel batch 1: frozen orders 2–4

The first three-candidate batch retrieved design materials concurrently and committed decisions centrally in frozen order. No worker extracted or opened participant response files. Search previews or over-broad source text exposed aggregate result-bearing material for each candidate; the exposures were logged without using the results, and all three candidates are barred from untouched evaluation independently of their design exclusions.

### `brandtS1`: ineligible

This respondent-level 2×2 vignette crosses whether a fictional household's foreclosure onset was controllable with whether its continuation was controllable. The four cells describe different target situations rather than interventions one decision-maker can assign to a stable household or population. Recasting the cells as communication frames would introduce an unsupported truthfulness constraint and an operational objective absent from the fielded instrument. Decision: `ineligible`, reason `scenario_target_attributes_not_deployable_actions`.

The instrument contains fixed 1–7 post-treatment measures of perceived control, responsibility, anger, sympathy, government-help support, and personal assistance likelihood. Government-help support would be the first terminal policy-relevant candidate only under a separately declared assistance-advocacy objective, but outcome selection cannot repair the action-set failure. Explicit assignment documentation, the study-specific weight, missing codes, and the module denominator also remain unresolved.

### `patriot_act`: ineligible

This participant-randomized, two-wave design assigns 16 packages combining processing instructions with first-wave frames or opposing first-to-second-wave frame sequences. Patriot Act support is measured on a 1–7 scale after treatment at both waves. The full arm set mixes pro- and anti-Act persuasion goals, artificial processing instructions, and sequences whose carryover is the estimand. It therefore lacks one stable decision-maker and monotone utility across all arms. T1 cannot be treated as a pretreatment baseline, T2 attrition requires a separate estimator, and the available supplement does not reconstruct every stimulus exactly. Decision: `ineligible`, reason `no_coherent_full_action_set_or_defensible_stable_utility`.

A sponsor-specific restricted sequence contrast could be reconsidered only as a non-test extension after exact stimuli, beneficiary, endpoint, utility direction, contrast, and attrition estimator are prospectively declared.

### `Melin1066`: ineligible

This participant-randomized 2×3 vignette crosses a fictional woman's prior occupation (military or cosmetics) with the target job context (automotive, wedding/events, or consulting). Those factors change the applicant's biography and the underlying job/market rather than deployable interventions applied to a common target. Salary has opposing applicant and employer utilities, masculinity has no defensible monotone direction, and another outcome cannot repair the action-set failure. Decision: `ineligible`, reason `factorial_target_attributes_no_deployable_action_set_or_stable_utility`.

The questionnaire and codebook verify bounded salary and bonus sliders, percentile judgments, 1–7 capability/reliability measures, randomized item order in several blocks, and a general-population post-stratification weight. These features make the study reconstructable as a factorial perception experiment, but not as a headline InterveneBench decision task.

## `Bougher893`: ineligible

Source title: *Issue (Dis)agreement and Intergroup Bias in Affective Polarization*.

Files inspected:

- OSF node: `cgbn5`
- Proposal/manuscript PDF SHA-256: `469f74c0ea1acd568a964467b5633d1fe9af63b395ab1950d27dc0895f0a4d15`
- Materials archive SHA-256: `d7ba7a81cdc31d87805487efb70105c7455d04dbc04394c8b2de23611dd4e8b9`
- Final programmed questionnaire SHA-256: `75e8933d6b67d3976e832033191cbd31d9984b88228e5298fe1a6773b3a1e8b9`
- Codebook SHA-256: `18a29617523c888e8cd5df0614ba7e3b94e195225fd8be9a4dda2235f4843331`

Verified design:

- Participant-level random assignment among nine nominal cells formed by three party-cue configurations and three issue-profile configurations.
- The party factor shows two candidates with no party labels, two candidates from the respondent's own party, or one own-party and one opposing-party candidate.
- The issue factor shows one candidate aligned with the respondent on all four issues or cross-pressures the two candidates by agreement on salient versus non-salient issues.
- The stimuli are personalized using each respondent's own party and issue positions.
- Candidate column, issue order, and candidate-to-agreement-profile assignment are additionally randomized. The source planned to pool some nominal cells because they present the same underlying comparison.
- Post-treatment measurements include paired candidate feeling thermometers, party feeling thermometers, and paired candidate vote-likelihood items.

Decision-task assessment:

The nine cells are not nine alternative interventions available to one stable decision-maker. They jointly alter candidate party composition and participant-relative candidate positions. Those are attributes of the hypothetical contest and participant-candidate match, not merely deployable messages or policies. Candidate A and Candidate B are also not stable beneficiaries because the agreement profile is randomized across their labels.

A future analysis could study factorial causal effects or define an explicitly normative composite such as reduced affective distance. That would answer a different estimand and would require a declared public-welfare utility, beneficiary, pooling rule, and participant-attitude access regime. It cannot be admitted as a primary InterveneBench decision task merely by treating the nine experimental cells as interchangeable actions or by selecting whichever candidate rating is convenient.

Decision: `ineligible`, reason `target_attributes_and_no_stable_decision_utility`. No participant data file was extracted or opened, and no human response value or treatment effect informed the decision. Because published result text was incidentally visible, `outcome_access` is conservatively recorded as `result_text_exposed_ineligible`; the study is barred from any untouched evaluation stratum.

### Accidental result encounter

The public TESS landing page and the proposal/manuscript PDF contain result-bearing text. Some of that text became visible while locating and OCR-checking the source materials. Candidate ordering was already frozen, and the exclusion above follows only the final questionnaire, codebook, and action-set rule. The encountered results were not used for eligibility, prioritization, outcome selection, utility construction, or any simulator decision. This incident also changes the retrieval procedure for later candidates: inspect questionnaire, codebook, and fielding materials first; avoid result summaries and manuscript prose unless the candidate is already ineligible or the exposure is explicitly acceptable for a non-held-out role.
